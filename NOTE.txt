TECH DEBT — Remove bokeh eviction patch
========================================

File:    analysis/fcs_io.py
Function: _ensure_plugin_bokeh()
Also:    __init__.py (BioPro metadata shim block)

WHY IT EXISTS
-------------
BioPro bundles a stripped-down bokeh inside its app bundle at:
  /Applications/BioPro.app/Contents/Frameworks/bokeh/

That copy is missing Jinja templates (e.g. file.html.jinja) that
flowkit needs to function. Because BioPro (or another plugin) imports
bokeh at startup, it gets cached in sys.modules pointing at the wrong
location. Our _ensure_plugin_bokeh() patch detects this and evicts the
bad bokeh before flowkit imports it.

HOW TO REMOVE IT
----------------
Once BioPro Core is fixed, delete:

1. The entire _ensure_plugin_bokeh() function in analysis/fcs_io.py
2. The _ensure_plugin_bokeh() call at the top of _load_with_flowkit()
3. The "BioPro metadata shim" block in __init__.py (lines marked with
   the ── BioPro metadata shim ── comment)

THE CORRECT CORE FIX (tell the BioPro Core team)
-------------------------------------------------
Option A (preferred): Don't bundle bokeh in the BioPro app Frameworks
at all. It is only needed by plugins, not by BioPro Core itself.

Option B: Bundle the COMPLETE bokeh (including all Jinja templates)
so plugins that depend on it don't get a broken import.

Option C: In module_manager._inject_plugin_path(), also scan
~/.biopro/cache/packages/ and insert those dirs at sys.path[0] BEFORE
the app Frameworks, so plugin packages always win over bundled ones.
